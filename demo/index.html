Getting Here

The Library sits on the bluff above Medora, North Dakota, a ten-minute walk from downtown and forty minutes east of Dickinson.

Parking

Complimentary parking is available in the east lot. Accessible spaces are nearest the Breezeway entrance.

The Evening

  • 5:30 PM — Reception on the Breezeway
  • 6:15 PM — Remarks from David Hume Kennerly
  • 7:00 PM — Exhibition opens

What to Wear

Business casual. The Breezeway is open to the Badlands air, so bring a layer — evenings on the bluff run cool even in October.
